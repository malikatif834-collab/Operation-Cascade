And when one is compromised, the cascade begins before anyone sees it.

This is not a description of the future.
It is a description of today.

IV The Response — Operation Cascade

Operation Cascade is a G7-level agentic supply-chain threat simulation. It is co-developed with peer-reviewed research, timed to the G7 Evian Summit of two thousand and twenty-six, and is designed to produce — not findings, but governance guidance.

The coalition is convened deliberately narrow. The intent is not breadth; it is operational standing.

  • Federal government
  • Critical-infrastructure operators
  • Academic institutions
  • International research bodies

The coalition is assembled.
The exercise date is set.
The gaps remain.

V Earn Your Seat

Participation in Operation Cascade is not open enrollment. The exercise is designed for institutions with operational standing in the sectors it models. The Evaluation Committee considers each application against the three questions that follow.

  1. i

    Who are you?

    Your institution, your mandate, your stake in the problem.

  2. ii

    What do you bring?

    Operational data. Research capacity. Simulation expertise. Governance authority. What does your participation make possible that is otherwise impossible?

  3. iii

    Why does this matter to you?

    Not a credentials summary. Tell us what is at stake — and why your organization needs to be in the room when this exercise runs.

Applications are reviewed by the Operation Cascade Evaluation Committee.
Selected participants are contacted directly. There is no waitlist.
